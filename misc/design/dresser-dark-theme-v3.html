Dark Theme Mapping Rules
How light → dark translation works for every element

Backgrounds

#FFFFFF → #121212 (main bg)
#F5F5F5 → #1E1E1E (cards/surfaces)
#FAFAFA → #252525 (elevated cards)
#E8E8E8 → #2A2A2A (surface accents)

Text

#1A1A1A → #F0F0F0 (primary)
#666 → #A0A0A0 (secondary)
#999 → #666 (tertiary)
#BBB → #555 (placeholder)

Borders

#F0F0F0 → rgba(255,255,255,0.08) (subtle)
#E8E8E8 → #333 (solid)
Dashed borders: #DDD → #444

Primary Button Inversion

Light: bg #1A1A1A, text #FFF
Dark: bg #F0F0F0, text #121212
The button inverts completely — always maximum contrast against the screen background.

AI Banner Inversion

Light: dark banner on light bg (black card)
Dark: light banner on dark bg (white card)
The banner always contrasts with the background — it's the visual highlight of the home screen.

Accent Colors (Brightened)

Sage: #5C8A6E → #6DAF8A
Blush: #D4726A → #E0877F
Steel: #6B89A8 → #85A3C0
Gold: #C4985A → #D4AD6A
Lavender: #9B8BB4 → #B4A3CC
All accents increase lightness ~15% for readability on dark.

Frosted Glass (Dark)

Light: rgba(255,255,255,0.9) + blur 8
Dark: rgba(30,30,30,0.7) + blur 8
Hearts, badges, bookmarks all use dark frosted glass on dark theme.

Toggle Switch

Light ON: bg #1A1A1A, knob #FFF
Dark ON: bg #F0F0F0, knob #121212
Light OFF: bg #E8E8E8, knob #FFF
Dark OFF: bg #2A2A2A, knob #666

Score Badge Backgrounds

Light: rgba(92,138,110,0.12) sage tint
Dark: rgba(109,175,138,0.15) sage tint
Same pattern for gold and blush — slightly higher opacity on dark to maintain visibility.
