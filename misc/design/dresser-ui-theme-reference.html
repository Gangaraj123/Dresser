6. Design Rules Summary

Elevation
No drop shadows anywhere. Use background color differences and 0.5px borders to communicate depth. White cards on cream background.
Borders
Always 0.5px solid with the border color variable. Never thicker unless it's a selected/active state (2px accent for selected style cards).
Animations
Subtle and purposeful. Toggle transitions: 0.2s. Page transitions: slide from right. Loading: shimmer effect on placeholder cards. No bouncing or elastic effects.
Images
Garment photos always have 3:4 aspect ratio, 14px radius. Product images on white background with subtle shadow. Use shimmer loading placeholders.
Safe Areas
Bottom nav: 28px bottom padding for home indicator. Status bar: respect system insets. Content: 20px horizontal padding on all screens.
Dark Mode
Warm dark, not cold. Background is dark brown (#1A1815), not pure black. Accent shifts to #D4935E (lighter copper). All semantic colors stay the same.
